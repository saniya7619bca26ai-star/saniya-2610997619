College Terminology

Syllabus
An outline of the subjects in a course of study or teaching.
Credit Hours
A unit that gives weight to the value, level, or time requirements of an academic course.
GPA (Grade Point Average)
A standard indication of a student's academic achievement and overall performance.
Prerequisite
A required course or topic that must be completed prior to taking a higher-level course.
Semester
A half-year term in a school or college, typically lasting 15 to 18 weeks.
